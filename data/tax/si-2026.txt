# MM-41 tax schedule: Slovenia, 2026 (plača: bruto - neto)

# What it covers: the pay of an employee -- social security contributions,
# the obligatory health contribution (OZP), the general allowance with its

# it leaves out: reimbursements of meals and travel, the holiday allowance
